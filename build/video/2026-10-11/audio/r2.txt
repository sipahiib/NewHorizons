What if fish could report the pollution we cannot see? Study photos show blue damselfish; other reef footage is context. Researchers studied blue damselfish at eighteen coastal sites in Okinawa. Urbanisation was associated with changes in gene activity linked to immune and inflammatory responses. That suggests fish counts alone may miss physiological stress. But the study is observational, covers one species, and does not prove a single pollution cause. These are changes in gene activity, not genetic mutations. The researchers see potential for environmental monitoring, with more validation needed.
