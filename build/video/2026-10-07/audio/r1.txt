An AI agent can click buttons. Can it follow the rules? OpenAI and Ironclad tested agents on eleven research tasks involving contract workflows and approvals. GPT six Astra averaged fifty-five percent of rubric requirements, compared with forty-one point six percent for GPT five point six Sol. That is a score, not the share of tasks fully completed. Estimated time savings were simulated, not measured in customer work. This company evaluation used different reasoning settings, and human oversight still matters. Announced October sixth, these findings show progress with clear limits. Follow for more science.
