A wildfire can build its own thunderstorm. Scientists in the INSPYRE campaign used aircraft and ground instruments to study fire-generated clouds over western North America. These clouds can drive dangerous winds and lift smoke high into the atmosphere. NASA's October second report describes summer fieldwork, including an August encounter over Idaho. The flights collected smoke particles, gases, and measurements of radiation. The next step is combining the measurements with satellite observations and models. Better warnings for firefighters are a goal, not a proven result. Follow for more Earth science.
