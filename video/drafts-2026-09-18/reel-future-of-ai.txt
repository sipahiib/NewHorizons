ChatGPT just moved into Word—and it can rewrite the sentence you select.

OpenAI says its Microsoft add-in places ChatGPT in a sidebar beside your document. You can turn notes into a draft, summarise what is already on the page, revise selected text, and adjust headings or formatting without leaving Word.

It is available across ChatGPT plans, including Free, but installation and sign-in are required. Token limits, shared allowances and workplace administrator restrictions may apply.

And remember: this is a product announcement, not proof that every answer or edit is accurate. Review generated text before you accept it.
