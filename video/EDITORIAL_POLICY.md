# NewHorizons Editorial Policy

## Story package

Select one visually compelling lead story with broad relevance and one second story. Balance artificial intelligence, technology, medicine, planet Earth and space across successive cycles. Source stories across the United States, Europe and the Far East excluding China across successive cycles; every region need not appear in each two-story episode.

Prioritise important developments that teach the audience something new or have practical or technical value. Avoid repeating a recent topic unless there is a materially important new development.

Every story must answer:

1. What happened?
2. Who did it or supplied the evidence?
3. Why does it matter?
4. How could it affect the viewer?

Explain practical relevance without promising unproven benefits. Limit each topic to one important financial figure or point.

## Evidence

Prefer reliable primary sources: papers, institutional releases, official datasets and first-party product announcements. Record direct links, publication dates and underlying event or paper dates separately. Clearly distinguish results from interpretation, projections and speculation.

State material limitations, including research stage, sample or model limits, uncertainty, missing outcome evidence and whether an announcement is newer than the underlying paper. Product claims are announcements unless independently demonstrated.

## Proposal and approval

For a new main-video cycle, propose two stories plus two independent Reel candidates: one for **Future of AI** and one for **The Planet Earth**. Proposing two Reels does not authorise producing both; produce one Reel for each user-approved topic.

Present together:

- concise story summaries and viewer relevance;
- primary sources and dates;
- claim limitations;
- realistic motion candidates and usage conditions;
- several English hooks for the main story and each Reel.

The candidate set must support the story timing and visual targets in `PRODUCTION_SPEC.md`, including its Reel segment count and flexible still/motion mix. Identify direct footage, contextual footage and possible still fallbacks before approval; final rights and technical audits still occur after approval.

Record research in the cycle's `RESEARCH_PACKET.md` and the user's selections in `APPROVAL.md`. Approval must identify topics, factual framing, hooks and the direct/contextual visual approach. Prior-cycle approval never applies to new content.

Use natural English for narration, headings and calls to action. Do not use “First of our news is.”
