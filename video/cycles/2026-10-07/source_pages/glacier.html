During an ESA-led expedition to the Gorner Glacier system in Switzerland, ESA astronaut Luca Parmitano joined glaciologists and climate experts to learn about how rising global temperatures are taking their toll on glaciers. This trailer offers a glimpse of what they saw; the full documentary will be released during the 26th UN Climate Change Conference of Parties in November.
