## YouTube title
Swift’s Final Descent & AI Hospital Handover Research

## YouTube description
A spacecraft that chased the universe’s brightest explosions is coming home. After almost 22 years in orbit, NASA’s Swift Observatory is expected to re-enter Earth’s atmosphere. We look at how Swift helped astronomers study fleeting cosmic events and why re-entry predictions can change.

We also explore a Stony Brook study comparing AI-generated and clinician-written hospital discharge summaries. The results are promising, but both approaches contained errors and human review remains essential.

NASA’s re-entry estimate in this video reflects its 2 November 2026 update. Check the mission blog for the latest information. Some telescope and hospital footage is contextual.
