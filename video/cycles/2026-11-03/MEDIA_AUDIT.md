Manifest records file hashes, exact shot durations, seek points and direct/contextual classifications. Source contact sheets and preview frames are under build/video/2026-11-03/. All assets are current-cycle downloads; no prior-cycle footage is reused.

Real, different telescope, contextual; spoken disclosure precedes use. Time-lapse, not Swift. A faithful left-side crop removes prominent wall signage; see manifest for native dimensions.
- `nasa-link-excerpt.mp4`: [NASA SVS LINK rocket integration](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/15061/), credit NASA/Ron Beard. Actual footage of Swift's rescue spacecraft, not Swift itself. The cycle file is a 20-second video-only stream-copied excerpt beginning at approximately 02:00 of the source; subject shown on contact sheet. The selected span begins eight seconds into this excerpt and uses a faithful central crop to remove vehicle logos and wall signs. Its file hash identifies the downloaded excerpt, not the entire 2.2 GB source.
- `nasa-link-test.jpg`, `nasa-link-pegasus.jpg`: [NASA Swift Boost Media Resources](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/swift/swift-boost-mission/swift-boost-media-resources/), credits NASA/Scott Wiessinger and NASA/Ron Beard, respectively. Actual rescue-mission preparation; not Swift. Both native downloads 1280 pixels wide.
