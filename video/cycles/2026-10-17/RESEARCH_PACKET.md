## Evidence, relevance and limits

VERID maintains a live laboratory representation, checks simulated actions and records real robot runs. Google reports recovery from a reader failure and replanning when a pipette would not fit a trough. These are case studies, not general reliability guarantees.

A separate aptamer prototype used experimental feedback over three rounds on two targets. One round favoured indiscriminately sticky molecules; measurements exposed this shortcut and informed revised designs. Viewer lesson: convincing AI output and useful experimental results are different achievements.

Protein-binding research does not establish a safe or effective cancer treatment. Avoid clinical efficacy, cure or approved-drug claims. People authorise and review sequences, verify protocols and supervise runs. The three projects are building blocks; fully integrating them remains a next step. Do not imply a complete autonomous discovery machine already exists.

Potential relevance: research tools could help scientists explore candidates and avoid wasted experiments. General cost savings and clinical benefits remain unproven. No financial claim proposed.
