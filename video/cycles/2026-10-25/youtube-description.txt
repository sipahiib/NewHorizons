What happens when an AI agent has to follow your company’s rules?

Japan’s Sakana AI announced Sakana Aquarium on 23 October 2026: a platform designed to connect organizational data, business rules and AI-agent workflows. Its proposed approach combines shared definitions of company information, access controls, human approvals and records of agent decisions and actions.

We look at the difference between an AI that answers a question and an agent authorized to act—and why knowing who can approve the next step matters.

These are capabilities described by Sakana AI. The announcement does not establish independent performance results or guarantee error-free operation.
