## Candidate: Sakana Aquarium — giving AI agents company rules

Japan’s Sakana AI announced Aquarium on **23 October 2026**. It describes a platform for enterprise agents built around shared definitions of organizational data, rules and relationships. Governance adds access controls, approval before execution, and records linking evidence, decisions and results. Managed Agents handles workflows. These are vendor descriptions, not independently measured results.

**Viewer relevance / editorial interpretation:** how does an assistant move from answering questions to taking actions at work while remaining accountable? Common definitions and approval responsibilities can matter as much as model answers. A hypothetical purchasing request could explain the concept, explicitly described as an example rather than a product demonstration.
