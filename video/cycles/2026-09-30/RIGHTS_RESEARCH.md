# Media rights research — 2026-09-30

No purchase or external permission request has been made. The production spec requires actual recorded footage and sourced stills in the main programme. This file records source-level findings; item-level provenance, hashes, dimensions, selection and visual correspondence belong in `MEDIA_AUDIT.md` and cycle manifests.

## M1 — ESA Juice

Four selected ESA stills are listed with exact URLs and SHA-256 values in `esa_stills.json`: the 28 September JMC Earth view, NavCam Africa view, NavCam Moon view, and a 21 September trajectory diagram. Their individual pages state **CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO or ESA Standard Licence**. Retain credit, link to licence, indicate any layout crop, and identify the trajectory as a diagram. The JMC and NavCam photos document the actual 2026 flyby. They are stills, not motion clips.

ESA's 2026 flyby videos found so far are animations, which `../../PRODUCTION_SPEC.md` excludes as main-story imagery. A 2024 Juice flyby movie explicitly prohibits extracting parts without ESA authorisation, and no request is authorised. Do not use it. Real recorded contextual footage, visibly labelled with its date/location, is still required for M1.

## Resolved production choices after independent review

The four selected ESA stills are used under each item's **CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO** option, with the page-specific ESA/Juice/JMC, ESA/Juice/NavCam, or European Space Agency/ATG Europe credit in `CREDITS.md`. They are fit/cropped into the frame; that adaptation is disclosed. The corresponding ESA pages present this licence as one of two permitted choices. The separate ESA Standard Licence is not the selected basis. [ESA use terms](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Terms_and_conditions_of_use_of_images_and_videos_available_on_the_esa_website).

M5 uses two independent [Pexels photographs](https://www.pexels.com/license/), not frames of its motion clips; item pages and creators are in `pexels_photos.json`. R1 and R2 use original, source-attributed documentary text cards. Those cards reproduce factual statements from the [Anthropic study](https://www.anthropic.com/research/your-thoughts-on-ai) and [Rome declaration](https://www.esteri.it/en/sala_stampa/archivionotizie/comunicati/2026/09/dichiarazione-ministeriale-dellupm-sullacqua-nel-mediterraneo/) with original typography; they do not reproduce the copyrighted page graphics or claim to be screenshots. The downloaded Anthropic hero and Italian ministry artwork are not selected in a manifest because broad reuse rights were not verified.
