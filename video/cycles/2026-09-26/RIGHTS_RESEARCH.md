# Media rights research — 26 September 2026

Scope: public licence evidence only; no downloads, contact, purchases or permission requests. “Unresolved” means no sufficient express grant was verified, not a finding that statutory exceptions cannot apply. No fair-use clearance is asserted.

## Google R1 — unresolved

The [Gemini Cloud announcement](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/gemini-3-8-live-with-live-avatar-is-now-generally-available/) exposes images/demos but has no visible CC notice. [Google Developers policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/site-policies) applies where its licensing notice appears; do not transfer it to this blog. [Media guidance](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/use-cases/books-and-media/) permits plain-text product references, requires trademark attribution/disclaimer, and discusses fair use separately. This is not a copyright licence for copying, excerpting or cropping the demos. [General guidance](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/) allows linking and built-in embedding; neither clears incorporation into an MP4.

Alternative: licensed recorded human/computer interaction with explicit “contextual footage—not Gemini demonstration” labels and original factual captions. That cannot demonstrate Live Avatar itself; replacing all promised direct demos materially changes the approved Reel visual treatment and needs controller assessment/approval before rendering.

Feasible alternative: [Markusp74’s own photograph of the Bedretto tunnel entrance, 16 August 2010](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eingang_Stollen_Bedretto_Fenster.jpg), CC BY-SA 3.0. Credit author/source/licence, identify alterations, satisfy ShareAlike for adaptations; label “Tunnel entrance, 2010—historical context.” It is not FEAR 3 imagery. Licensed recorded tunnel/instrument footage remains necessary.
