Arctic sea ice doesn’t just follow the wind—the pieces keep crashing into each other. Engineers at UC Riverside built a collision-based model for ice moving through Fram Strait. Using local conditions, it reproduced how quickly ice spreads, its speed distribution, and changes across measurement timescales. The idea is closer to floating grains that drift, push and collide than to a sheet passively following the air. This could improve how larger models represent ice transport.
