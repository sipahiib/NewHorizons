China's PALLAS-1 rocket has reached orbit on debut. The liquid-fuel vehicle uses seven engines in its first stage and is designed for a future that includes vertical landing and reuse. This debut did not attempt a recovery, but reaching the intended orbit validates major parts of propulsion, guidance, and staging. The next test is harder: turning one successful launch into reliable operations, then proving a returning booster can land and fly again. Follow for more space technology.
