Try this safe experiment with an adult: fill a glass with water, cover it with a stiff card, hold the card firmly, and turn the glass upside down over a sink. When you carefully release the card, outside air pushes upward while the water pulls downward. If the seal holds, air pressure can support the card and the water above it. Science is not only something inside a laboratory. With simple materials, careful observation, and adult supervision, invisible forces become visible at home.
