Complex life just broke a heat record.

Scientists found an amoeba in California that reproduces at sixty-three degrees Celsius.

It came from a hot spring in Lassen Volcanic National Park.

Incendiamoeba cascadensis remains active at sixty-four degrees. That raises the known heat limit for cells with a nucleus.

This does not prove complex life exists elsewhere. Survival also requires suitable water, oxygen, pressure, food and an ecosystem.

But the discovery expands the conditions in which complex cells can function—and widens the search for life.
