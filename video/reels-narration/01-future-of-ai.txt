What if classroom AI helped students think—without taking over the thinking? Indiana University researchers will help lead a renewed five-year, twenty-million-dollar NSF institute with university and nonprofit partners. The goal is to study AI that supports choices made by learners and teachers, instead of simply producing answers for them. That could mean tools that keep educators in control. But this is a research programme, not proof that finished products already improve learning. AI cannot replace a teacher, and funding does not guarantee results.
