ChatGPT just moved into Word—and it can rewrite the sentence you select.

OpenAI says its Microsoft add-in places ChatGPT beside your document. It can turn notes into a draft, summarise the page, revise selected text, and adjust headings or formatting.

It is available across ChatGPT plans, including Free, but installation, sign-in and token limits apply. Workplace administrators may restrict access.

And remember: this is a product announcement, not proof that every answer or edit is accurate. Review generated text before you accept it.
