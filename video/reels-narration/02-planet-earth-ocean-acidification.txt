A more acidic ocean shrank squid brains by nearly half in a controlled study.

Researchers kept bigfin reef squid for ninety days in present-day seawater or conditions simulating ocean acidification in 2100. Academia Sinica reports brain volume was forty-nine percent lower, and optic lobes fifty-two percent smaller.

Body size showed no clear difference. Basic visual responses remained, although hunting performance and neural metabolism changed.

This was one species in a controlled scenario. It does not prove wild squid have changed or that every marine animal responds the same way.
