Does your running form change from fatigue—or simply because you sped up?

Researchers tested twenty-one male runners at three treadmill intensities while cameras measured movement in three dimensions. Changes linked to speed and intensity were more consistent than changes building up with time.

So when your stride looks different late in a hard run, fatigue may not be the only explanation. The faster pace itself can alter movement.

This was a small, male-only treadmill study, and speed and metabolic demand could not be fully separated. It is useful evidence, not an injury diagnosis or a universal running prescription.
