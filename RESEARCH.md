# Research Brief

For each new cycle, prepare a two-story technology and science news package from the latest three days. Source stories across the United States, Europe and the Far East excluding China; maintain this geographic scope across successive cycles, without requiring every region in one episode. Balance artificial intelligence, technology, medicine, planet Earth and space across successive cycles; two stories need not cover every field in one episode. Prioritise important, educational and practically useful developments, and avoid repeated topics unless there is a material new development.

Verify claims with current reliable sources and present the candidates and source links for user selection. Follow `video/EDITORIAL_POLICY.md` for the proposal and approval format.

On `/start`, create a new `video/cycles/YYYY-MM-DD/` directory even when an older cycle still has pending delivery work. Write research to its `RESEARCH_PACKET.md`; do not erase, overwrite or silently resume an earlier cycle. Update `video/SOURCES.md`, `video/WORKFLOW.md` and the `video/CREDITS.md` pointer for the new active editorial cycle while preserving older cycle records.
